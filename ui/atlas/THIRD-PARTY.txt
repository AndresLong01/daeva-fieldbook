The 5,665 gathering coordinates and 48 field-boss locations in this
prototype were transformed from
the public Aion2Data client-data snapshot (AION 2 Global client 2.0.6.0
hotfix 1, Steam build 25800038): https://aion2data.com/about
Only resource and boss names and coordinates were retained. The app does not
contact a map website at runtime.

The Abyss tab's six schedules are based on the Global client schedule tables
described at https://aion2data.com/timers (EventSchedule/AbyssEvent and
WorldMapFieldNamed/PeriodSpawn). Its 15 boss and Artifact map positions were
transformed from the same client-data map snapshot. The countdowns are
calculated locally, not fetched from that website. Client schedule times do
not establish live boss status, and Siel's clock should be checked in game.

When the Timers tab is available, the app makes a read-only JSON request
once a minute to the community field-boss feed at aion2timers.com for
NA West Siel. Its reports come from players and may be delayed or missing.
The map, gathering data, notes and local timer fallback work without it.
The 48 feed ID mappings were checked against the open-source
MythosOfs/aion2-tracker project (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 MythosOfs):
https://github.com/MythosOfs/aion2-tracker
